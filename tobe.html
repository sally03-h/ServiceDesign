How to read this map

  • The to-be is a proposal. Nothing here has been tested with customers or staff. Each phase says how sure we are of the problem it answers: cited the problem is evidenced; inferred the problem itself is assumed; hypothesis the fix has a known tension.
  • The as-is comes from our P5 skeleton and the seven gaps (C1–C7) on the ecosystem map. Evidence codes resolve to the A3 repository in index.html.
  • Emotion scores are team estimates on a 1–5 scale, not measurements. We have no post-visit data for any persona.
